# AMR Awareness Initiative

The **AMR Awareness Initiative** was run under **Bioliberty (Global Accelerator Program)** to combat Antimicrobial Resistance (AMR) through evidence-based public health interventions.

## Major Deliverables

- **Behavior-Change Design**: Designed and deployed a targeted educational program reaching 600+ high school and university students across multiple international institutions.
- **Academic Publication**: Co-authored and produced a peer-reviewed research paper (published in **Wiley, 2024**) detailing the effectiveness of behavior-change methodologies.
- **Remote Collaboration**: Coordinated deliverables across a geographically distributed team of researchers, educators, and science communicators.
