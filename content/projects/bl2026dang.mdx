# AMR Awareness Campaign Conducted at Shahid Krishnasen Ichchhuk Polytechnic Institute

**Promoting Responsible Antibiotic Use Among Future Healthcare Professionals**

**On March 29, 2026**, an AMR Awareness Campaign was successfully held at Shahid Krishnasen Ichchhuk Polytechnic Institute, Ghorahi Sub-Metropolitan City–17, Gairagaun, Dang. The event brought together 2nd- and 3rd-year Diploma in Pharmacy students, along with faculty members, including the Head of Department, for an engaging and informative session on one of today’s most pressing public health concerns.



The session was led by Rabi KC and Manish Rijal, Biotechnology graduates from Kathmandu University, Dhulikhel. Through their presentation, they highlighted the growing challenge of antimicrobial resistance, the importance of responsible antibiotic use, and the need for informed decision-making in healthcare practices.




As future healthcare professionals, the participating students gained valuable insights into how misuse and overuse of antibiotics can contribute to resistance, making infections harder to treat. The program served as an important step in encouraging awareness, responsibility, and better practices for a healthier future.
