# HIV Awareness Campaign

**Educating and Empowering Youth at Shree Rajaji Janjagaran School**

We are thrilled to announce the successful completion of our HIV awareness campaignat **Shree Rajaji Janjagaran School,** where we engaged with 70 enthusiastic studentseager to learn about HIV prevention, stigma reduction, and overall health awareness. 
This impactful initiative was made possible through a meaningful collaboration between'We' for Change, UNAIDS-Nepal, and our dedicated team atYouth Activism Nepal (YAN). Together, we aimed to spread essential knowledge about HIV/AIDS, debunk myths, and empower students with the right information to create a more informed and supportive community.
During the session, students actively participated in discussions about HIV transmission, prevention, and the importance of early diagnosis. 


Through interactive learning methods, we emphasized the need to combat stigma, promote safe health practices, and encourage a supportive environment for those affected by HIV.
We extend our heartfelt gratitude to the school administration for their unwavering support in facilitating this program. A special thanks to the incredible volunteers from Youth Activism Nepal (YAN), whose dedication and passion played a crucial role in making this event a resounding success.
This campaign is part of our ongoing commitment to youth empowerment and health education. By equipping young individuals with accurate knowledge and fostering open discussions, we are taking vital steps toward eradicating misconceptions and promoting a healthier society. 


The fight against HIV/AIDS is not just about awareness—it's about building a future where every individual is informed, supported, and empowered. Let’s continue working together to break the stigma and spread knowledge for a healthier tomorrow!
