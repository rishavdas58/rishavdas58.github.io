# John Galt School Nepal 2026 – Coming Soon


## John Galt School Nepal 2026 – Coming Soon
**Think. Question. Lead.**

John Galt School Nepal is an intensive philosophy and leadership program for young people who are eager to develop critical thinking, intellectual independence, and principled leadership. Through guided discussions, readings, debates, and interactive sessions, participants explore the ideas that shape free societies, personal responsibility, innovation, and human flourishing.

Designed for ambitious students, young professionals, and emerging leaders, the program challenges participants to think deeply, question assumptions, and engage with timeless philosophical concepts while developing practical leadership skills for the real world.

Participants will learn from experienced lecturers, collaborate with peers from across Nepal, complete thought-provoking assignments, and become part of a growing network of young leaders committed to reason, liberty, and positive social change.

**Why Join John Galt School Nepal?**

Develop critical thinking and analytical skills.

Explore philosophy, ethics, economics, and leadership.

Engage in meaningful discussions with outstanding mentors and peers.

Build a nationwide network of future leaders.

Strengthen your ability to lead with reason, integrity, and confidence.

Applications for **John Galt School Nepal 2026** will open soon.

Stay tuned and get ready to be part of one of Nepal's most unique intellectual and leadership development programs.
