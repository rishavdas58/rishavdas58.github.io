# Plantation 2025

**Plantation For A Greener Tomorrow**

Youth Activism Nepal is committed to building a resilient and sustainable environment by empowering young people to lead local climate action. Through our dedicated plantation drives, we have planted 1500+ trees in protected and monitored areas across Saptari, Ithari, Kathmandu, and Janakpur  ensuring long-term survival and safety of every sapling. Each plantation is carried out in partnership with schools, local authorities, and community leaders, engaging students, teachers, and residents in environmental responsibility. These efforts help expand urban green spaces, improve air quality, protect biodiversity, and reduce climate-induced risks. By encouraging youth to nurture the trees they plant, we are cultivating a generation that values sustainability, stewardship, and a greener future for Nepal. Every tree symbolizes hope  a commitment today for a healthier tomorrow.
