# Project Repurpose

**Project Repurpose** is a flagship initiative of **Youth Activism Nepal** focused on environmental sustainability, waste management, and fostering a circular economy in Nepal.

## Key Outcomes

- **Nationwide Scale**: Active campaigns and waste management frameworks operationalized across 15 geographic districts of Nepal.
- **Community Engagement**: Organized workshops and cleanup programs educating 10,000+ local beneficiaries.
- **Circular Economy Models**: Partnered with local municipal offices and recycling vendors to repurpose plastic wastes, converting them into useful consumer items.
- **Cross-functional Coordination**: Managed a distributed volunteer team to drive local execution.
