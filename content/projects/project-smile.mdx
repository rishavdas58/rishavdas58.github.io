# Project Smile

**Project Smile** is a continuous public health campaign launched in 2017, designed to provide oral hygiene education and high-quality dental care products to marginalized communities.

## Core Milestones

- **1,500+ Beneficiaries**: Reached over 1,500 children and community members with free dental screening camps and hygiene packs.
- **Sustainable Impact**: Conducted long-term monitoring on behavioral change related to hygiene practices in rural districts.
- **Collaborations**: Partnered with local dentists, dental colleges, and private donors to source equipment and healthcare volunteers.
