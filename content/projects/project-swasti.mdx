# Project Swasti

**Project Swasti** was established under the **U.S. Embassy Youth Council** to empower rural women through artistic skill development and Mithila cultural heritage preservation.

## Key Program Achievements

- **Cultural Preservation**: Organized public Mithila mural painting campaigns to revive traditional artwork and foster community pride.
- **Empowerment Through Art**: Trained 50+ local women in painting and craft skills, helping them establish sustainable micro-businesses.
- **US Embassy Partnership**: Collaborated closely with U.S. Embassy program officers and community organizers to design culturally sensitive programs, aligning with gender equity and heritage goals.
- **Outreach**: Reached over 500 community members through public engagement and cultural exhibition events.
