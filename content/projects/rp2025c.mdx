# Repurpose-Chitlang

**In the serene hills of Chitlang, at Shree Sochan Bhairavi Secondary School,**

In the serene hills of Chitlang, at Shree Sochan Bhairavi Secondary School, students explored how plastics can be reused instead of burned a common harmful practice in rural areas. Their repurposed products are now used in classrooms, reducing waste while encouraging innovation. Local families praised the initiative for helping preserve their village greenery.
