# Reprupose-Gorkha

**In the historic hills, at Ratna Rajya Secondary School**

In the historic hills, at Ratna Rajya Secondary School, Project Repurpose helped students understand how unmanaged waste contributes to floods and landslides. They built useful items from plastic bottles and learned civic responsibility. The youth now see sustainability as a shield that protects both their heritage and future.
