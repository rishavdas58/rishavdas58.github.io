# Repurpose -Muglin

**At Triveni Baraha Secondary School, students pledged to reduce road-side waste**

Mugling is a busy transit hub, yet sustainability rarely takes center stage. At Triveni Baraha Secondary School, students pledged to reduce road-side waste by introducing repurpose activities in school events. They are now ambassadors for cleanliness along major national highways  small leaders in a big logistical town.
