# Repurpose- Mustang

**At Shree Janhit Secondary School, students surrounded by towering mountains**

At Shree Janhit Secondary School, students surrounded by towering mountains learned how even small plastic waste can harm their fragile ecosystem. They repurposed bottles into planters and tool holders, creating a culture of creativity and responsibility. Teachers celebrated how students took ownership of their environment, proving that change can start in the most remote corners.
